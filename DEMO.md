# Orator — 90-Second Demo Script

Total: ~90 seconds. Everything below uses hands-free samples (no live mic
needed on stage). Sound ON. Use Chrome/Edge. AI Coach selected.

## 0:00–0:10 — The hook (Speech)
1. Open the app. Point at the journey line:
   **Speak → Feedback → Retry → Targeted exercise → Debate → Profile**.
2. Press **Connect** (status: Connected; system line reads Speech ready).

## 0:10–0:35 — Practice → Feedback → Retry → Comparison
3. **Start attempt** → **▶ Sample 1** → wait for the final transcript →
   **Finish**. Point at measured metrics + retry focus (not generic praise).
4. **Try again** → **▶ Sample 2** → **Finish**. Point at the comparison:
   *what improved, what stayed the same, what to focus on next.*

## 0:35–0:55 — Adaptation (the differentiator)
5. Scroll to **Up next — assigned for you**: the app names YOUR weakest
   metric and prescribes a drill. Say: *"It doesn't treat sessions
   independently — it adapts the next exercise."*
6. Flash the **Profile** panel: strengths, recurring weaknesses, trends.

## 0:55–1:20 — Debate (training partner, not chatbot)
7. **Debate Coach** tab → AI-education motion → Start → **Speak argument** →
   **▶ Sample argument** → Finish → read the opponent's attack on YOUR
   specific weak point → **Diagnose** → scorecard.
8. Closing line: *"Same engine, third mode"* → click **Interview Coach** tab
   (don't demo it, just show it exists).

## 1:20–1:30 — Close
*"Three coaches, one training loop: practice, diagnose, retry, measure —
with the next challenge chosen by the system, not at random."*

## If something fails on stage (say it out loud, don't hide)
- **Token/401 at Connect:** "The hosting key didn't reach this deployment —
  that happened to us once; the status line tells you before you connect."
- **No transcript:** "AssemblyAI streaming hiccup — reconnect and replay the
  sample; samples make every failure retryable in 15 seconds."
- **AI slow/fallback badge:** "That's the honest fallback — Rules Coach,
  never a fake AI answer. The badge always tells the truth."
- **Mic dead:** use the samples; the entire demo above needs no microphone.

## Pre-demo checklist (5 min before)
- [ ] Status line reads *Speech ready* (else: env keys + redeploy).
- [ ] Sound ON (samples are heard + captured).
- [ ] AI Coach selected (or demo the Rules fallback on purpose).
- [ ] One full dry run under 100 seconds.
